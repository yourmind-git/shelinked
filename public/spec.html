SheLinked

MVP Build Specification

AI-powered career acceleration platform for women

Version 1.0 — MVP

North Star

SheLinked helps women accelerate their careers and become stronger leaders, product managers, executives, consultants, and entrepreneurs through personalized assessments, coaching, mentoring, and career roadmaps.

Primary Goal: Help users make measurable career progress.

Core Principle: When making trade-offs, optimize for user outcomes and coaching quality, not feature quantity.

Target Users

Future LeadersTeam LeadsManagers Scrum MastersProduct OwnersProduct Managers ConsultantsEntrepreneurs

MVP Features

1. Authentication

Sign up, sign in, sign out. Protected routes, session persistence, mobile responsive.

2. Career Profile

Collect role, industry, experience, goals, desired role. Save to database, editable, powers all AI features.

3. Leadership Assessment

10-question questionnaire → score, promotion readiness, strengths, growth areas, recommended role.

4. AI Career Coach

Conversational coach using profile + assessment context. Identifies obstacles, recommends actions, creates accountability.

5. Career Roadmap

Generates 3/6/12-month plans, skills to develop, networking strategy, promotion strategy, success metrics.

6. AI Mentor

Four personas: Executive Leader, Product Leader, Startup Founder, Agile Coach. Personalized, consistent, context-aware.

Explicitly Out of Scope

  • Community feeds / forums
  • Social networking
  • Event systems
  • Corporate dashboards
  • Talent marketplace
  • Learning management system
  • Video / course platform

These will later be integrated through HyperMind.

Product Principles

📱 Always mobile first. Desktop second.

🤖 Always AI first.

🎯 Every page should answer: "What should the user do next?"

⚡ Prefer action over information. Coaching over content. Simplicity over complexity.
